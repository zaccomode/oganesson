# Typography

Oganesson offers a standard suite of typography styles.

_See also: Typography.sketchframe_

## Large Title

Large titles are designed largely for stylistic choice: they are impractically large for most UI scenarios, but may be used in roomy areas or where the developer wishes to heavily emphasise content.

## Standard Title

The standard title for a page, typically equivalent to a `h1`. Like with `h1`, there should only be one standard title visible on a page or modal at a time.

## Second-Tier Title

A smaller title for a page, functionally equivalent to a `h2`. Many second-tier titles may exist on one page.

## Label

The label text style is used for paragraphs, labels and placeholders across applications. It is the most common form of text style in Oganesson.

## Small Label

Small labels are used for secondary or supplementary content where the label text is too large, but using a subtitle or caption would strain the user's eyes.

## Subtitle

Subtitles are used for secondary or supplementary content. All text using the subtitle style should use Geist Mono regular in uppercase with the secondary foreground color.

Subtitle and caption text should generally be avoided for paragraphs longer than a short sentence because of their uppercase mono style. Where a paragraph of supplementary content is required (e.g. an input description), consider using a small label instead

## Caption

Captions are used for select circumstances where a subtitle is too large for the scenario, such as for secondary content on a button.
